# Batch 4 — Furious portrait production brief

**Status:** The eight genuine Batch 4 Furious portrait paintings are **not yet approved or installed**. Composite marketing/poster attempts that introduced different leaders or house names were rejected and are not included in the game.

## Non-negotiable visual contract
- Create **eight standalone square character portraits**, not a collage, poster, banner or mock-up.
- Same identity and species as the existing source portraits in `public/art/avatars/*_expansion_neutral.png`; do not replace with generic stock science-fiction characters.
- Facial emotion: distinct **furious / enraged / indignant** expression with visible eyebrow/eye, jaw/mouth, and body tension. Expression must still read at 64px.
- Preserve the hair, facial proportions, anatomy, costume vocabulary and established house colours. No baked-in lettering, logos, watermarks, status tags or borders.
- Head-and-shoulders, centred and tightly framed, 1:1 source at least 1024px square, realistic cinematic lighting.
- Do not imply the character has changed faction, species, name or game role.

## Eight characters and identity anchors
1. **Commander Kaia Rho (`kaia`)** — silver short-haired human military commander, tailored red-black armour; furious at a broken order, cold controlled anger.
2. **Zib-Zob (`zibzob`)** — olive green Glorp mechanic with large eyes, pointed ears and instrument goggles, technological exosuit; agitated, fierce scowl and gritted teeth, still recognisably the same creature.
3. **Matriarch Oolu (`oolu`)** — pink Coralite elder with branching living-coral headdress and pearlescent ceremonial armour; stern maternal fury, furrowed brow, not a new humanoid species.
4. **Vex Halloran (`vex`)** — rugged dark-haired human smuggler with cheek scar and black tactical coat; snarling defiance, eyes locked on an opponent.
5. **Archon Pyrrhus (`pyrrhus`)** — volcanic fire-skinned Solari warlord in gold armour; incandescent eyes and blazing, enraged expression.
6. **Dr Nim Okafor (`nim`)** — Black human woman scientist with natural dark curls and purple-light futuristic research suit; determined anger at a disastrous result, not a different scientist.
7. **Skrill (`skrill`)** — bright blue chitinous insectoid queen, organically patterned exoskeleton with angular crown/crest; antennae and mandibles express insectoid fury.
8. **Captain Bramble (`bramble`)** — green Floran botanical being with leafy hair, yellow flowers and natural wood-grain skin; tangled, fierce facial expression, leaves bristling.

## Target runtime files
After approval each of the eight expressions must be prepared as 512×512 PNGs under these three filenames:
`<id>_frontier_furious.png`, `<id>_expansion_furious.png`, and `<id>_ascension_furious.png`.
These can use approved era treatments derived from eight source paintings, but any derived treatments must be described as such, not 24 independently illustrated new faces. The existing fallback displays the neutral portrait of that era until the Furious file is ready.

## Art QA
- Identity check side-by-side with the current Expansion Neutral master.
- Visual expression check at 64×64 and 128×128.
- No new names, houses, emblems, settings or game UI embedded in source art.
- Image format audit and TV/PC/phone manual review.
