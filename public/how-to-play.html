One screen, two jobs: the TV displays the shared star map and results. Your phone is your private command console for planning, resources, Missions, Draft picks and trade offers.

What happens every round?

1

Solar Storm & income

The two-hex-wide Solar Storm moves one hex. Worlds caught in it normally earn no resources this round; owned hyperlanes still pay Credits. Watch the outlined forecast for next round.

2

Plan together

Everyone secretly enters up to two commands on their phones, optionally ranks the public Draft Pool, plays held cards and can trade. The timer is 60 seconds in Quick or 90 seconds in Standard/Advanced. Hit Engage.

3

Reveal & resolve

Players receive their preferred available Draft card in turn order. Everyone's first command resolves, then second commands, and then Strikes. Completed Missions, trophies and victory are checked at the end.

Your six commands

Most actions spend resources you collect by owning worlds. If an order is no longer possible when it resolves, it fails and refunds 1 Credit.

Five things you collect

Ore

From Rock Worlds. Used for Outposts, Spires and hyperlanes.

Ice

From Ice Worlds. Used to found Outposts.

Crystal

From Crystal Worlds. Used for Spires and Research.

Plasma

From Gas Giants. Used for Spires and hyperlanes.

Credits

From Trade Stations, Spires and hyperlanes. Used for Fleets, Research and trades.

Bank trading

Exchange three of one resource for one of another during Plan. Offers to other players require their acceptance.

How Influence works

Earn points

A Trade Station Outpost is worth 1, a Spire 2, and a Megaspire 4. These replace the previous value rather than stacking. Secret Missions award 2–4 Influence. The Longest Network and Grand Armada trophies are worth 2 each.

The finish line

Quick: first to 10 Influence. Standard / Advanced: first to 15. Victory is checked at the end of Reveal; simultaneous finishers are compared by total Influence. Otherwise the highest score after the final round wins.

Need more detail?

How do I set up my first Outposts?

After choosing a house, take turns in a snake order (forward, then backwards) to place two starting Outposts. Each starts with one Fleet. Choose production worlds: Rock, Ice, Crystal or Gas. The TV shows legal choices.

How do Draft cards work?

At the start of Plan, the TV reveals one more Draft card than there are players. Rank the cards on your phone. In turn order, each player receives their highest-ranked still-available card. Some activate immediately; others can be held or grant Influence while their condition holds.

What happens in a Strike?

Select your own source sector with Fleets, then an adjacent enemy sector outside the storm. Both sides roll one six-sided die and add their Fleets and bonuses. The defender wins ties. A winning attack captures the sector; defenders that survive retreat if possible. Losing does not eliminate you from the game.

Why is part of the galaxy striped?

It's the Solar Storm. It moves one hex each round and normally stops resource production in affected worlds. You cannot Strike a sector inside it. Its faint outline shows where it will be next round. Owned hyperlanes continue earning Credits.

What's different in Advanced mode?

Advanced adds the eight house perks and Cosmic Events. You also lose 1 Influence for each unfinished Mission at the end of the game. For your first match, Standard is the easier choice.

Can I play on PC or without a TV?

Yes. Open the TV/Host screen in a browser window on a computer, laptop or tablet. Other players open the phone controller on phones or browser tabs and join using the displayed code. Keep the TV/Host window open.

Will I hear sound?

TV sound is off initially: choose Sound off · Enable at the bottom of the TV game and adjust Volume. The phone has its own optional Sound off control for quieter feedback. Both require a tap or click before audio can play. You can preview samples in the Sound Lab.
