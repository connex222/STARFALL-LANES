When a star died, empires awakened.

About this lore: this is new, original fictional background written for the game. The collapsed star, Starfall Cluster, Solar Storm, three eras and eight leaders come from Design Doc v2; the narrative details below expand that setting rather than change its rules.

A brief history of the Cluster

Before the Fall

The forgotten routes

Old navigational records show a scattering of Trade Stations linked by fragile hyperlanes, with a silent Beacon at their centre. Nobody agrees who built the first stations. Every house claims to have a fragment of the original map.

The Starfall

The night the star collapsed

A bright star folded into darkness and scattered energy across the sector. Ore-bearing rock, ice, crystal and plasma-rich worlds were revealed in its aftermath. What first looked like the end of a system became the beginning of a new frontier.

The Frontier Era

First lights on new worlds

Small landing parties arrived under rival house banners. First came Outposts and supply runs. The Beacon remained neutral, a fixed point in an otherwise shifting galaxy.

The Expansion Era

Trade becomes power

Stations rose into Spires. Couriers began to flash down hyperlanes, while fleets patrolled contested borders. Alliances were brief, but no house could build everything alone.

The Ascension Era

Who will become Warden?

The Cluster grew brighter as Megaspires pierced the void. The Solar Storm still crossed the board, indifferent to titles and ambitions. The title of Warden awaited whichever house could turn expansion, diplomacy and daring into lasting Influence.

“The stars do not belong to us. Yet every house has drawn its border around them.” — from an anonymous frontier dispatch

THE SOLAR STORM

The galaxy does not stand still

Storm light walks across the hexes like an approaching tide. Veterans learn to read the faint warning it leaves one round ahead. Settlers shelter in their domes while hyperspace couriers keep moving.

The storm is not a villain. It is the Cluster's heartbeat — powerful, predictable for a moment, and impossible to own.

THE BEACON

The centre holds

The Beacon is the one sector no house can claim. Routes converge towards it, but nobody owns its light. Among the captains it has become a symbol of what the Cluster could become: connected without being conquered.

Whether the Beacon is a relic, a warning or a promise remains an open question for future chapters.

YOUR CHAPTER BEGINS

Make your own history

Every game regenerates the galaxy, places the Solar Storm on a new approach and deals new Missions. The lore gives the houses their history; the choices at your table determine what happens next.
